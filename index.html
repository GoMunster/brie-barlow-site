The Realm

What you'll find in these pages

If you want your fantasy with forbidden love, a magic returning, and kingdoms who must reckon with these changes, then this world is for you..

  • Magic

    Ancient and mostly lost, but flows through the earth like water and only felt by those gifted to it.

  • Crystals

    The physical formation of the magic once used in imbuements but now lost to time and war.

  • Swords

    Duels at dawn, training-yard sparring, and blades with memories.

  • Mythical beasts

    Another loss to time and war, but with a path back to this world opened by someone on our side.

  • Romance

    Slow burns that earn their fire. Guaranteed happily ever after for the main pair.

  • Fully realized women

    Heroines who fight, but love fashion. They are more than a single note.

  • Royalty

    Crowns that turn out to be much heavier than they look and require great sacrafice.

About Brie

Hello, I'm Brie.

I write the books I wanted when I became a mother: big, swoony fantasy where the heroine doesn't stop being the hero once there's a baby in the story.

My stories are full of crystal magic, sword fights, mythical creatures and royal courts, but at their heart they're about love in all its shapes: the romance that sweeps you off your feet, and the fierce, unglamorous love that gets up for the midnight feed.

When I'm not writing, I'm reading, sketching, painting, or anything that could be perceived creative. Also, I have 3 chickens and a corgi. Oh, and a husband and three kids.

Founding Readers

Help me build the kingdom

My first romantasy is still being written, and I'd love a small circle of readers along for the ride. Founding Readers get a letter from Illarune about once a month, and:

  • Chapter 1 before anyone else
  • Votes on real story details: names, gowns, chapter titles
  • First chance to be an advance (ARC) reader
